Q: What is the primary requirement for a connected graph to be considered Eulerian?
?
A: Every vertex in the graph must have an even degree.


Q: In Fleury's Algorithm, which type of edge should you avoid choosing unless no other options exist?
?
A: You should avoid choosing a "bridge" (or cut-edge)—an edge whose removal increases the number of connected components in the graph.


Q: Where must you start Fleury's Algorithm if the graph contains vertices of odd degree?
?
A: You must start at one of the vertices with an odd degree.


Q: What is the objective of the Chinese Postman Problem?
?
A: To find a closed walk of minimum length that traverses every edge of the graph at least once.


Q: If a graph has $2k$ vertices of odd degree, how many disjoint open trails are needed to cover all edges?
?
A: $k$ disjoint open trails.
