Q: What is the Problem of the Seven Bridges of Königsberg?
?
A: It is a problem that asks whether you can find a walk that begins in a land region of Königsberg, crosses every one of the 7 bridges exactly once, and returns to the starting region.

Q: How did Euler model the Königsberg bridge problem to prove a trail doesn't exist?
?
A: He modeled it as a graph where the vertices represent the land regions and the edges represent the bridges.

Q: What is the definition of an Eulerian trail?
?
A: An Eulerian trail is a trail that passes through all the edges of a graph.

Q: What makes a graph Eulerian?
?
A: A graph is Eulerian if it possesses a closed Eulerian trail.

Q: According to Theorem 2.1, what is the condition for a connected graph to be Eulerian?
?
A: A connected graph is Eulerian if and only if each of its vertices has an even degree.

Q: Based on Corollary 2.1, when does a connected graph have an Eulerian trail?
?
A: It has an Eulerian trail if and only if it has at most two vertices of odd degree.

Q: How can a connected graph with 2k odd degree vertices be decomposed?
?
A: It can be decomposed into k disjoint open trails.
